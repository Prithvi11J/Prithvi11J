### ⭐ Latest: LaunchScout

<a href="https://github.com/Prithvi11J/launchscout-competitor-crew">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Prithvi11J/launchscout-competitor-crew/main/docs/demo_thumbnail.png" alt="LaunchScout demo" width="640">
</a>

**Know your market before you sign the lease.** A crew of **4 AI agents** researches local competitors for a new business idea: their real prices, their Google reviews, the gap in the market, and a 30-day launch plan. Every price, rating and quote links to the page it came from.

- Full competitor report in **about 2 minutes**
- Python checks every agent's answer: a price counts only if a page shows it next to the business's name
- Caught **158 of 161** planted errors (98%), and the test exposed a real bug that is now fixed (5/21 → 21/21)
- In thin markets it stops instead of guessing

**[▶ Try it live](https://launchscout-demo.streamlit.app)** · **[Code & demo video](https://github.com/Prithvi11J/launchscout-competitor-crew)**

### ⭐ Featured: ReviewLens

<a href="https://github.com/Prithvi11J/reviewlens-feedback-analyzer">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Prithvi11J/reviewlens-feedback-analyzer/main/docs/demo_thumbnail.png" alt="ReviewLens demo" width="640">
</a>

Turns a week of restaurant reviews in **English, Tamil and Tanglish** into a 5-minute Monday briefing, a priority reply queue and a weekly report, built for a fictional three-outlet Chettinad kitchen in Coimbatore.

- Tags **240 reviews in about 45 seconds**
- Checked against a separate answer key: **98% category, 99% sentiment, 100% urgency**
- Found all **4 hidden problems** planted in the data, including a food-poisoning spike at one outlet
- Numbers come from code and words from the LLM, and a pattern is only reported if it beats chance

**[▶ Try it live](https://reviewlens-demo.streamlit.app)** · **[Code & demo video](https://github.com/Prithvi11J/reviewlens-feedback-analyzer)**

`LangChain` `OpenAI` `pandas` `Streamlit` `57 tests`
